# Artwork

Files: [banner](banner.png) (1300×372, the Nexus mod-page header size) · [square cover](thumbnail.png) (1254×1254, packaged in the release ZIP; Arsenal and HD2MM show it).

Drawn in code with Claude Opus 5.5 (Pillow and NumPy); no image generator was used.

- Type: Franklin Gothic Heavy for the title, Bahnschrift SemiBold (width 87) for the menu.
- Colours: ground `#121110`, cream `#f0e8d6`, yellow `#fdd00c`.
- The menu shows real content: the example options from the README's "For mod authors" section, and on the cover Shallow Water Diving's Max Dive Water Depth slider.
- On Nexus the lower left of the header sits under the page's title and stats, so everything that must read is above y≈200.
- The PNGs carry no metadata chunks.
